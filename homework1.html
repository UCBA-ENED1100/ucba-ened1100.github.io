Homework 1: Introduction to Engineering Design

Assignment Overview

Due Date: TBD

Points: 100

Submission: Submit via Canvas


Learning Objectives

  • Understand the engineering design process
  • Identify different engineering disciplines
  • Apply problem-solving techniques to real-world scenarios

Assignment Tasks

Part 1: Engineering Disciplines Research (40 points)

Research three different engineering disciplines and write a one-page summary for each that includes:

  • Overview of the discipline
  • Typical career paths and job roles
  • Required skills and education
  • Example projects or applications

Part 2: Design Process Application (40 points)

Choose an everyday problem and apply the engineering design process to propose a solution:

  1. Define the problem
  2. Conduct background research
  3. Specify requirements
  4. Brainstorm solutions (at least 3)
  5. Select the best solution and justify your choice
  6. Create a preliminary design sketch

Part 3: Reflection (20 points)

Write a one-page reflection addressing:

  • What you learned about the engineering design process
  • Which engineering discipline interests you most and why
  • How this assignment has influenced your understanding of engineering

Submission Guidelines

  • Use 12-point font, Times New Roman or Arial
  • Double-spaced, 1-inch margins
  • Include a cover page with your name, date, and assignment title
  • Submit as a single PDF file
  • File name format: LastName_FirstName_HW1.pdf

Grading Rubric

Criteria Points
Part 1: Discipline Research 40
Part 2: Design Process Application 40
Part 3: Reflection 20
