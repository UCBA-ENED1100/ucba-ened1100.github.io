Late Work Policy

Late assignments will be accepted with the following penalties:

  • Up to 24 hours late: 10% deduction
  • 24-48 hours late: 20% deduction
  • 48-72 hours late: 30% deduction
  • More than 72 hours late: Not accepted without prior approval

Exceptions will be made for documented emergencies or university-approved absences.


Attendance Policy

Regular attendance is expected and may affect your participation grade. Students missing more than 3 classes without valid excuse may see their final grade reduced.


Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. Collaboration is encouraged on group projects, but individual assignments must be completed independently. Violations of academic integrity will result in a failing grade for the assignment and may result in further disciplinary action.
