University Policies

Academic Integrity Policy

The University of Cincinnati is committed to academic integrity. All students must adhere to the University's Academic Integrity Policy. Acts of academic dishonesty include, but are not limited to:

  • Cheating on exams or assignments
  • Plagiarism or improper citation of sources
  • Unauthorized collaboration on individual assignments
  • Fabrication of data or information

For more information, visit the UC Student Code of Conduct.


Disability Services

Students with disabilities who need academic accommodations should contact the Accessibility Resources office. Once registered, please provide your instructor with an accommodation letter as soon as possible.


Title IX and Non-Discrimination

The University of Cincinnati does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, sex, disability, religion, age, sexual orientation, or veteran status. For more information or to report discrimination, contact the Office of Gender Equity & Inclusion.


Mental Health and Wellness

Student mental health and wellness are important. If you are experiencing stress, anxiety, or other challenges, please reach out to UC Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS). Services are free and confidential.


Emergency Procedures

In the event of an emergency, follow instructions from campus security and emergency personnel. Familiarize yourself with evacuation routes and emergency procedures for your classroom building. For emergency notifications, sign up for UC Alerts.


Recording of Classes

Recording of class sessions is not permitted without prior written permission from the instructor. Recordings are for personal use only and may not be shared or distributed.
