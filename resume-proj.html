Resume and Portfolio Project

Project Overview

Due Date: TBD (End of Semester)

Points: 150 (15% of final grade)

Team Size: Individual

Submission: PDF files via Canvas


Project Description

This project requires you to create a professional engineering resume and portfolio that showcases your skills, experiences, and projects completed during the course. These documents will be valuable tools for internship and job applications.


Learning Objectives

  • Develop professional communication skills
  • Learn to effectively present your qualifications and experiences
  • Understand what employers look for in engineering candidates
  • Create a portfolio that demonstrates your technical abilities
  • Prepare for the co-op and job search process

Project Components

Part 1: Engineering Resume (70 points)

Create a one-page professional resume that includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone, email, LinkedIn profile
  • Education: University, degree program, expected graduation, GPA
  • Technical Skills: Software, programming languages, tools
  • Projects: Course projects (including LEGO project), personal projects
  • Experience: Work experience, internships, volunteer work
  • Activities: Student organizations, leadership roles, honors

Part 2: Engineering Portfolio (80 points)

Create a portfolio document (3-5 pages) that includes:

  • Cover Page: Professional title page with your name and contact information
  • About Me: Brief professional biography and career objectives
  • Project Showcase: Documentation of 2-3 significant projects including:
    • Project description and objectives
    • Your role and responsibilities
    • Technical approach and methodology
    • Results and outcomes
    • Photos, diagrams, or screenshots
  • Skills and Competencies: Detailed list of technical and soft skills

Resume Guidelines

  • Use a clean, professional format with consistent formatting
  • Limit to one page (strictly enforced)
  • Use action verbs (designed, developed, analyzed, created, etc.)
  • Quantify achievements when possible
  • Proofread carefully for grammar and spelling errors
  • Use standard fonts (Times New Roman, Arial, Calibri) 10-12pt
  • Save as PDF for submission

Portfolio Guidelines

  • Professional appearance with consistent branding
  • High-quality images and graphics
  • Clear and concise technical writing
  • Demonstrate breadth and depth of skills
  • Include the LEGO Mindstorms project as one showcase
  • Save as PDF for submission

Project Timeline

Week Milestone
Week 11 Resume workshop and draft submission
Week 12 Peer review of resumes
Week 13 Portfolio workshop and planning
Week 14 Portfolio draft and peer review
Week 15 Final submission of resume and portfolio

Grading Rubric

Component Points Criteria
Resume Content 40 Completeness, relevance, and accuracy
Resume Format 20 Professional appearance and organization
Resume Writing 10 Grammar, spelling, and clarity
Portfolio Content 45 Project documentation and technical detail
Portfolio Design 25 Visual presentation and professionalism
Portfolio Writing 10 Technical writing quality and clarity
