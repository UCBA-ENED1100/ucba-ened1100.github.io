Quizzes

Quiz Information

Quizzes will be administered throughout the semester to assess your understanding of course material. All quizzes will be taken on Canvas.


Quiz Format

  • Number of Quizzes: 6-8 quizzes throughout the semester
  • Format: Multiple choice, true/false, and short answer
  • Duration: 20-30 minutes per quiz
  • Location: Online via Canvas (unless otherwise specified)
  • Attempts: 1 attempt per quiz

Quiz Schedule

Quiz dates will be announced at least one week in advance. Check Canvas regularly for updates.

Study Tips

  • Review lecture notes and slides before each quiz
  • Complete all homework assignments on time
  • Participate actively in class discussions
  • Form study groups with classmates
  • Ask questions during office hours if you need clarification

Make-Up Quiz Policy

Make-up quizzes will only be given for documented emergencies or university-approved absences. Contact the instructor as soon as possible if you need to request a make-up quiz.
